# v0.26 — PC and Android testing

Run `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, then `npx wrangler dev --local --port 8787`. Local accounts are separate from production.

## Android friend

Open the preview in current Android Chrome and reload after deployment. A fresh browser should select Mobile quality, show touch controls, and leave Photographic surfaces off. Existing saved preferences take precedence; change them in FIELD MENU if needed.

1. Enter in portrait. Check health, stamina, ammo, crosshair and all controls fit. Rotate to landscape and let the next rendered frame settle; confirm the camera aspect and controls resize correctly. Rotation clears held movement and aim/run toggles.
2. Move with the left stick. Small movements should walk slowly, full displacement should reach normal speed, and diagonals must not be faster. Drag the empty view to turn. Tap AIM and RUN to toggle them.
3. Switch to the rifle. Hold the movement stick while holding FIRE, then drag FIRE to turn. Release FIRE while keeping the movement thumb down: firing stops but movement continues. Release the stick: movement stops.
4. Tap RELOAD, USE, MELEE, PULSE and HEAL in appropriate situations. HEAL is disabled at full health; it still takes 2.6 seconds while stationary when available. A pistol requires separate taps; holding the rifle fires automatically. On defeat, tap RESTART.
5. Open JOURNAL. Inspect five species, select an owned companion, or track an unowned one. Close it and check distance/direction guidance remains visible. Open BACKPACK and FIELD MENU; confirm you can scroll and close them without firing or walking.
6. Hold movement/fire, then switch away from the browser and return. Neither should remain held. Repeat after rotating, losing a touch, and opening a menu. Co-op continues while a tab is hidden.
7. Try Mobile, Performance and Balanced to compare appearance and responsiveness. Only enable photo textures if the device and connection can handle them. Leave Cinematic for more capable hardware. Preferences should survive reloading this browser.

Check on the actual phone for frame rate, heat, battery use, safe areas, browser-toolbar changes and the on-screen keyboard. Emulation cannot verify those. iOS Safari is not the primary tested target for this release.

## PC and co-op

1. PC should default to mouse/keyboard and Balanced. Capture the mouse and verify movement, aim, both weapons, reload, melee, interaction, creature Pulse and healing.
2. Compare walk/run phases, knees/feet, relaxed arm motion, bandaging and infected wind-up/fall. Bodies should stop taking hits immediately and disappear after the short fall/fade.
3. Watch creature wings, tails and ground gaits. In two different accounts in the same room, check the other player's melee, Pulse and bandaging presentations. Repeated blocked actions must not spam cosmetic broadcasts.
4. Visit forest and wetland. Check curved fronds, falling leaves, cloud drift and water ripples. Switch Mobile → Balanced → Cinematic → Mobile; every tree must retain a crown, full detail must restore, and postprocessing must release when leaving Cinematic.
5. Resize, leave/rejoin rooms and clear several waves. Check for shader errors and resource growth. Reload and confirm saved progression is preserved.

## Validation boundary

Automated coverage includes joystick dead zone/analog speed limits, leg geometry, stance/swing continuity, preset bounds and server gating of cosmetic actions, plus the existing combat/account/survival suite. Chromium touch testing uses Android Chrome emulation with actual multi-touch dispatch. Render checks cover animation poses, remote motion, corpse cleanup, and thinning/restoration of tree matrices/colors. These checks do not measure physical-device frame rates or production multiplayer load.

Photo-render checks may serve previously downloaded, hash-verified original bytes through browser test routes to isolate rendering from the managed proxy. Live CDN delivery remains a deployment-origin check as documented for v0.25.
