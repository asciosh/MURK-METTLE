# v0.24 — Five-species field journal

## Collection and evolution

- **Mossback → Briarhide → Elderroot** lives on the southern pine ridge at X 48, Z 38. Its pulse is a slow, powerful close-range attack: 62 base damage, 5m range, 12s cooldown.
- **Stormrook → Thundermantle → Tempestcrest** lives near the eastern relay at X 54, Z -46. Its pulse favors distance and frequency: 24 base damage, 13m range, 5s cooldown.
- Both species have original procedural silhouettes that change visibly at 120 and 400 XP. There are now five collectible species and fifteen visible forms. Evolutions happen automatically at the XP thresholds.
- The collection journal shows every evolution name, threshold, current pulse stats, and XP remaining until the next form. Evolution progress restarts within each stage and stays complete for final forms.
- Click an unowned creature's journal card to track its habitat. A gold map ring and distance/compass readout guide exploration. Click again to stop; bonding clears tracking automatically. Tracking marks the destination, not a safe walking route.
- Owned cards select your active companion; C continues cycling owned companions. New species use the existing E-to-bond and Q-to-pulse controls.
- Solo legacy Lanternkin XP is no longer credited when a different species lands a pulse. Collection XP remains independent for each species.

## Saves and multiplayer

Old three-species collections gain locked entries for the new species when read. Existing ownership, XP and valid active selections are preserved. No reset, migration-tag change or new Cloudflare binding is needed. The same normalization covers local collections, co-op accounts and restored room attachments.

Co-op still verifies habitat distance and ownership on the server. New species use the existing per-enemy reward identifiers and XP rules, including duplicate-reward protection. The journal and tracking work with the current mode's collection; solo and co-op progression remain separate as before. Reload all open tabs after updating. Older clients do not know the two new species.

The creatures are procedural stylized models, not photorealistic assets. All five currently use radial pulse attacks with different damage, range and cooldown; this update does not add elemental weaknesses or turn-based battles. Tracking is session-only. Existing solo/client authority and account authentication limitations remain.
