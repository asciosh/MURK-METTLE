# v0.24 testing

Run `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, then `npx wrangler dev --local --port 8787`.

1. Sign into an existing account and enter the field. Check that the previous three creatures retain their ownership and XP. The new two start unowned.
2. Expand **Collection & evolution**. There should be five cards. Inspect each evolution chain and the pulse damage, range and cooldown. The list scrolls within the panel.
3. Click Mossback. Check the gold ring at X 48, Z 38 on the minimap and its distance/direction readout. Click again to stop tracking, then track Stormrook. This changes tracking without switching an owned active companion.
4. Travel to a habitat, approach the creature, and press E. Check that it joins the collection, becomes active, and the tracking marker clears. Select other owned cards or press C to switch. Press Q near infected to use its pulse.
5. Earn XP with the active creature. At 120 XP its form changes; at 400 XP it reaches the final form. Check the evolution announcement, model silhouette, stronger pulse stats and journal progress. Normal kills grant 12 creature XP and pulse kills grant 40; solo also grants XP for surviving pulse hits under the existing rules.
6. Refresh/sign back in and check ownership, independent XP and selected companion. Tracking itself resets.
7. In two different accounts, join the same co-op room. Bond with a new species at its habitat and confirm the other player sees the matching companion. Trying to bond from far away or select an unowned species must fail. Confirm XP persists after leaving/rejoining.

Automated tests cover old-save normalization without mutation, invalid data, stage boundaries, reachable habitat positions, new-species ownership, independent progression to final form and duplicate reward protection. Existing combat, survival, account and room tests remain in the suite.
