# v0.23 testing

Run `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, then `npx wrangler dev --local --port 8787`. Use this complete Worker rather than the historical Node server. Local accounts are independent of production.

1. Register or sign in and enter the field. Check the group counter, multiple infected and all their red minimap dots. Shoot a distant infected and observe the group investigate.
2. Hold Shift while moving. Stamina falls, sprinting stops at exhaustion, and the bar recovers after releasing sprint. Walking remains available.
3. Approach an infected. A warning ring appears before its strike; retreat promptly to dodge. Take damage deliberately for the next check.
4. Stop safely and press H. Treatment takes 2.6 seconds, spends one bandage and restores up to 40 health. Check that reaching 100 health keeps your position. Repeat while walking or firing: treatment cancels without refunding the bandage.
5. Visit the camp supply cache at X -3, Z 3. Ammo and bandages refill. Repeating immediately cannot refill them again; wait 45 seconds.
6. Kill the whole group. Check the eight-second intermission and the next wave. Rifle shots must hit the closest intersecting enemy; solid buildings block shots and creature pulses.
7. Open two signed-in tabs with different accounts and join the same room. Check matching enemies, health and deaths, remote gunfire/reloads, and persistent kill rewards. Groups use party size at spawn; joining does not immediately add enemies to an existing wave.
8. In co-op, repeat stamina and bandage checks. Die, press R, and check that the survivor returns to camp with restored session supplies. Leaving the room starts a fresh solo encounter.
9. Refresh and sign back in. Account progression and creature ownership remain; combat resources restart for the new session.

Automated coverage includes stamina exhaustion/recovery, interrupted and capped healing, movement-budget rejection, safe bounded group spawning, cover/target selection, wind-up dodging, crowd damage grace, noise, routes, unique encounter IDs, delayed waves, and actual room message handling. Browser and local Worker checks supplement these tests; they do not establish production load or real-device frame rates.
